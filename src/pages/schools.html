You assign the work. {{PRODUCT}} shows you how each child is doing.

  • Assign letters, textbook exercises and tests
  • A record for each child, so your tutors spend their time teaching, not checking
  • Children practise in a safe space made for them, between your sessions
  • Works alongside how your centre already teaches
