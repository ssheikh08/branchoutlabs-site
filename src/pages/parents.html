What your child does with {{PRODUCT}}

  1. 1

    Letters first

    Vowels, consonants and the signs that join them, learnt through quick games, always ending in real words.

  2. 2

    Their textbook, chapter by chapter

    The words, meanings and exercises from the book they use at school, explained first and then practised.

  3. 3

    Writing, on screen then on paper

    They write each answer on the device, {{PRODUCT}} checks it letter by letter, and then they copy it into their notebook.

  4. 4

    A short test each week

    On what they practised that week, with written answers like a school test, so test day feels familiar.

Questions parents ask

Do I need to know any Kannada?

No. {{PRODUCT}} talks to your child in Kannada and English, and your view is in English.

Which textbooks?

{{PRODUCT}} is built on the Karnataka state Kannada textbooks for Grades 3–6, which many CBSE and ICSE schools in Bengaluru use. Tell us which book your child has. We're adding more boards and books as we go.

Does it replace my child's Kannada class?

No. It's practice at home for what they learn at school, from the same textbook.
