    07 · Fair questions

    Things parents ask us.

    Do I need to know any Kannada?

    No. Akka talks to your child in Kannada and English, and your view is in English.

    Does it follow my child's textbook?

    Akka is built on the Karnataka state Kannada textbooks for Grades 3–6, which many CBSE and ICSE schools in Bengaluru use. Tell us which book your child has. More boards are on the way.

    Is it a replacement for school?

    No. It's practice at home for what they learn in class, from the same book.

    What does it need?

    An Android tablet or phone with internet, a notebook and a pencil. iPhone and iPad are coming soon.

    What does it cost?

    We're starting with a small group of founding families. Get in touch and we'll explain how it works.

    What happens to my child's data?

    It's stored in India and used to teach your child. Read our privacy policy, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

    Who's behind Akka?

    Akka is made by Branch Out Labs, a small team in Bengaluru founded by Shoaib Sheikh. It started with one child struggling with a Kannada exam. When Shoaib realised how many families were stuck the same way, he decided to build something that could help. A former software engineer at Amazon, he now works on Akka full time.

    Today, Akka is shaped by feedback from teachers, education professionals and parents. You can always message Shoaib directly on WhatsApp.
